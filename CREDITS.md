# Credits and data sources

- **Boundaries, lakes and capitals (World map):** [Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/) 1:10m Admin 0 – Countries, Lakes and Populated Places. Public domain. Borders follow Natural Earth's de facto lines, which not everyone agrees with.
- **Flags (`flags/world/`):** downloaded from [Flagpedia](https://flagpedia.net/download), whose flag images are in turn based on Wikimedia Commons. Please check Flagpedia's and Wikimedia's current terms before redistributing. Many national flags are in the public domain, but a few images on Wikimedia carry their own licence conditions.
- **Sierra Leone and United States maps:** see the `credit` line in `map-settings/sierra_leone.js` and `map-settings/us.js`, and the header of each file in `maps/`.
- **Code:** MIT licence, see `LICENSE`.
