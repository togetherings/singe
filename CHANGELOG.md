# Changelog

SINGE uses `major.minor.patch` version numbers. Builds made before versioning (v001 to v024) are treated as 0.1.0.

## 0.4.1
### Transnistria, Abkhazia and South Ossetia
- **Three new places on the World "all" map:** Transnistria (capital Tiraspol), Abkhazia (Sukhumi) and South Ossetia (Tskhinvali), in every mode, with their flags (from Wikipedia, public domain) and a note in Practice mode. On the World (UN countries) map they are grey, like Crimea and Kosovo. The World "all" map now asks about 275 places (was 272); the UN map and the Africa maps are unchanged.
- `tools/world.py` cuts them out of Moldova and Georgia along the lines in Natural Earth 5.1.2's *Admin 0 – Disputed Areas* (`BREAKAWAY_CUT`, new required option `--disputed`), now kept in `data/natural_earth/`. Only the shapes around them change: Moldova, Georgia, and Ukraine's and Russia's borders with the new places (simplified in new pieces, so the shared borders still match).
- `tools/world.py` takes Crimea, Ceuta and Melilla by their outlines in the disputed-areas file instead of a box drawn round each, and cuts Western Sahara off Morocco along the file's line instead of between two points written into the tool. The map looks the same (one more point on Morocco's border, on the same straight line). Isla del Perejil (an uninhabited islet next to Ceuta, which the Ceuta box used to catch; too small to show) is now left out (`PIECE_DROPS`).
- The capital rule has one exception: Transnistria gets Tiraspol, though only Abkhazia and South Ossetia recognise it.

## 0.4.0
### Keyboard play, new colours, menus and maps
- **EduGame Kit 0.3.10.** The list of keys is the kit's (`EduGame.addKeys`): its ⌨ button now sits beside Options in the header on every page (the game bar's and the map menu's are gone), and it opens with ?, / and that button, with the menu's part first on a menu and the F row only in Flags mode. The first-key hint is the kit's too. The prompt, the right and wrong messages and the highlighted place are read out with `EduGame.say`. The information and big-flag panels use `EduGame.dialog`, and both menus' arrow keys `EduGame.arrowNav`; in Options, Enter on a choice applies. The browser test checks the list of keys with `edugame.testing`.
- **Menus:** the map menu's top line has the heading and "x of y done" (game modes, not Practice, whose goal is met), like the website's menu; the two lines under the heading are gone. Buttons are the same everywhere: an outline in the border colour and teal on hover, including Options and "Sets of maps" in the title bar and the buttons and choices in the Options panel, which now has the game's colours and font.
- **More keys:** / opens the list of keys too (in Firefox it opened Quick Find); ; skips (on a French keyboard it's next to , and . needs Shift); the 0 key shows the whole map without Shift on a French keyboard; F (or D, for drapeau) shows the flag bigger in Flags mode. On the menus the arrow keys go to the nearest button, Home and End to the first and last, and 1-9 open a set of maps on the website's menu. Back from a game puts the keyboard on the button of the game just left. The list of keys has the menu's keys first when opened on a menu, in one aligned list.
- **Fixed borders next to grey land:** a border between a country in the game and a grey one is drawn in the game's colour, Cyprus is grey all round on the Africa maps, and a lake's shore is grey where it lies in a grey country (a lake shared with one in the game is grey on the grey side only).
- **Somalia and Somaliland are one country, with no border between them,** on the World (UN countries) map and the Africa maps other than Africa all (`merge` in a map's settings: the shared border is left out of the outline, and the pointer follows the joined outline). On the World map Somaliland was grey before. World all and Africa all keep them apart.
- **The sea in the boxes is the map's sea:** the boxes round moved regions (Alaska, Hawaii, France's overseas regions), the boxes under the World map and the lakes were a shade darker than the sea around them.
- Africa all and World all: "including the small ones" is gone from their descriptions.
- **The site's menu** (the website's first page and the all-maps offline copy) now looks like the game's own map menu: the same colours, font, frame and cards, in light and dark.
- **A new game bar:** Back (was "Back to menu") at the top left, the Skip buttons in the middle, Time and Found (or Practice's totals) at the top right; the name to find alone and centred on the second line (no "Find:"; in Flags mode just the flag); the right/wrong message on the third. Zoom is a stack at the top right of the map: ⌂ (the whole map), +, −. Each line and the stack can sit above the map or lie over it, per map (`overlay` in its settings): by default the message and the zoom stack lie over the map, and on the World map everything does.
- **Keyboard play.** A map on screen has the keyboard: Tab and Shift+Tab move through the places (in a game only the ones not answered yet), the arrow keys go to the nearest place in that direction (Shift + arrows move the map), Enter or Space answers, + − Page Up Page Down zoom, Home or 0 shows the whole map, . > and , < are Skip and Back, ? lists the keys (also the new ⌨ button), and Escape closes a panel, then goes back to the menu. The highlighted place gets the pointing outline (and a ring around small places), the map scrolls to keep it in view, and it never names a place the learner hasn't found. Screen readers hear the prompt, the right and wrong messages and the highlighted place. In English and French.
- **New colours, light and dark:** contrast where it matters, calm colours elsewhere. Text is black or white on its background; borders are black (light) or white (dark), answered places included; answers are colour-blind-safe green, yellow and vermillion (Okabe-Ito and Paul Tol palettes) that also differ in lightness; the page and sea are a soft blue (light) or deep navy (dark); places not in the game are pale and fade into the background; menu cards and buttons are dark blue in the dark theme. Capitals mode shows countries in the plain land colour instead of green.
- **Pointing:** hovering or the keyboard turns an unanswered place white (grey in dark) and an answered one 15% lighter, and draws a thick outline round it above every other border (white edged with black in light, black edged with white in dark; a ring round a circle). The hint and the wrong-part flash get the same outline; the hint's flashes with its colour, so it never looks like an answered place.
- **Capitals fixed** where the map tools had picked a country's biggest city, the capital had moved, or the name wasn't in English: Myanmar (Naypyidaw), South Africa (Pretoria), Netherlands (Amsterdam), Côte d'Ivoire (Yamoussoukro), Tanzania (Dodoma), Burundi (Gitega), Benin (Porto-Novo), Sri Lanka (Sri Jayawardenepura Kotte), Kazakhstan (Astana), Palau (Ngerulmud), Palestine (Ramallah), Equatorial Guinea (Ciudad de la Paz, since January 2026), Western Sahara (Tifariti, the Sahrawi Republic's temporary capital), Copenhagen, Bogotá, Andorra la Vella and Washington, D.C. On the World and Africa maps; the circles are at the right cities. `tools/world_settings.py` names them (`OVERRIDE`).
- **Notes about capitals and former names** (Practice mode: click a country): countries with more than one capital name the others (Bolivia, Israel, Malaysia, Eswatini, Montenegro...), planned or recent moves are mentioned (Egypt's New Capital, Nusantara, Sejong, the Makran coast), and countries say what they used to be called (Burma, Ivory Coast, Turkey, Holland, East Timor, Swaziland, Zaire, Upper Volta, Dahomey, Rhodesia, Ceylon, Siam...). In French on the Africa maps.
- **Sierra Leone notes:** Bonthe District says Bonthe is its largest city and sometimes mistaken for the capital (Mattru Jong); Kpanguima and Bandajuma say they became districts in 2026, Falaba and Karene in 2017. In French too.
- **Fixed: Skip and Back no longer wipe wrong tries.** A place keeps its wrong tries (and its highlighted answer) when it's skipped and comes back, so it turns yellow or red as it should, and skipping can't be used to get three fresh tries.
- **Fixed: name labels stay on the screen.** A label near the right or bottom edge (a tap on a phone) opens on the other side of the finger, and wraps if it's wider than the screen.
- **United States: Flags mode**, with the flags of the 50 states, D.C. and the 5 territories (`maps/us/flags/`, from Wikipedia, public domain), including Utah's and Minnesota's 2024 flags.
- **More World flags** (from Wikipedia, public domain): Northern Cyprus, Crimea, Kaliningrad, Newfoundland (the flag of Newfoundland and Labrador, as its info says), Okinawa, Easter Island (Rapa Nui), Sakhalin, Sardinia, Sicily, Corsica, Tasmania, the Azores, the Balearic Islands, Saba and Sint Eustatius. Alaska and Hawaii use the US map's flags. The World "all" map has flags for 267 of its 272 places: not Crete, Baffin Island or the Galápagos, which have none of their own, and not Saint Martin or Svalbard, whose flags were France's and Norway's (so they aren't in its Flags mode any more).
- **World capitals follow the rule** (a territory has no capital unless another state recognises it): Saint Martin, Sint Maarten, Saba and Sint Eustatius lose theirs; Somaliland gets Hargeisa (Israel recognised it in December 2025).
- The US map's five territories use the World map's flags (one copy of each).
- **British Isles:** its menu card shows the UK's and Ireland's flags split diagonally (`maps/british_isles/flag.svg`).
- **Smaller flags:** every flag's metadata (Flagpedia's C2PA content credentials, Inkscape editor settings) is removed by `tools/clean_svg.py`: the World map's flags go from 6.9 MB to 4.5 MB. Every flag was rendered before and after: none changed. Tristan da Cunha's flag is optimised from 1036 KB to 496 KB, 31 state and territory flags (8.4 MB to 6.5 MB for all 56), and Corsica's, Sicily's, the Azores' and Sakhalin's the same way, each checked by rendering at 1600 px against its original (Tristan da Cunha's original is kept in `data/flag_sources/`).
- The France map's menu card shows the French flag (`maps/france/flag.svg`, the World map's, without its embedded metadata), like Sierra Leone's and the United States'.
- **France:** a new map in the Countries package, with France's 18 regions: the twelve of the Hexagon, Corsica, and the five overseas regions in boxes (Guadeloupe, Martinique and French Guiana to the west, Mayotte and Réunion to the south). Practice, Regions and Capitals (each region's prefecture), in English and French; no flags yet. The neighbouring countries are grey, and Suriname and Brazil are drawn beside French Guiana in its box. Made by `tools/france.py` from Natural Earth 5.1.2 (its departments merged into regions). Two new scoreboards.
- Projected maps (the US and France): the boxes of moved regions are opaque, and a shapes file can have grey neighbours (`off`), drawn under the boxes, or inside one (with an `inset`). `map_generation.py` leaves out a region too small to draw.
- **British Isles:** a new map in the Countries package, with 12 places: England, Scotland, Wales, Northern Ireland, Ireland, the Isle of Man, Jersey, Guernsey, Cornwall, the Hebrides, the Orkney Islands and the Shetland Islands, in every mode, in English and French, with France and the other neighbours in grey. Made by `tools/british_isles.py` from Natural Earth 5.1.2 (map units, and Cornwall's admin-1 border), at ten times the World map's detail. Three new scoreboards.
- World-type maps can be squeezed sideways (`x_scale` in the shapes file's projection), so the British Isles keep their true shape.
- **New flags** in `maps/world/flags/` (from Wikipedia, public domain): England, Scotland, Wales, Northern Ireland (the Ulster Banner, with a note that it hasn't been official since 1972), Cornwall, the Hebrides (the Western Isles Council's), Orkney and Shetland. The World map's "all" map uses the last three, so the Hebrides, Orkney and Shetland are now in its Flags mode.
- **Natural Earth 5.1.2** (the newest release; was 4.0.0): the World and Africa maps' borders are rebuilt from it. Borders and coasts are a little more detailed in places, and a few tiny islands come or go; every map asks about the same places as before. New, in grey on every map and never asked about: Bir Tawil (claimed by neither Egypt nor Sudan), Brazilian Island (Brazil / Uruguay) and the Southern Patagonian Ice Field (Chile / Argentina). The Cocos (Keeling) Islands now have a shape, not only a circle.
- **Natural Earth data in `data/natural_earth/`**: the countries, lakes and populated places files the World map's tools read, with a README. `tools/world.py` handles 5.1's outlines (drawn the other way round), and `tools/world_settings.py` picks Abuja for Nigeria.
- **Africa:** a new package, *Studies In Geography: Africa*, with seven maps: North, West, Central, East and Southern Africa (the African Union's regions, UN countries only), Africa (the 54 UN countries) and Africa (all countries and territories: also Western Sahara, Somaliland, Ceuta, Melilla, the Canary Islands, Madeira, Réunion, Mayotte, Saint Helena and Tristan da Cunha). Every mode (Practice, Regions, Capitals, Flags), in English and French, with neighbouring countries in grey. Western Sahara is grey and Somaliland is part of Somalia except on the last map. 21 new scoreboards.
- **Windows on the World map:** the Africa maps draw the World map's shapes (borders, circles of small places, lakes), so they look and work like it. A map's settings can name the regions asked about (`in_game`: the rest are grey), put more parts into a country in UN scope (`merge`), and leave out the boxes under the map (`insets: false`); the map opens on the regions in the game (`view_pad`). A package's map can draw another map's shapes (`"shapes": "world"`).
- **Ceuta and Melilla** are split off Spain (`tools/world.py`, and the same two pieces moved in `maps/world/shapes.js`): places of their own on the World map's "all" map and the Africa "all" map, and part of Spain in UN scope. Each is a city, so neither has a capital (like Vatican City).
- **New flags** in `maps/world/flags/` (from Wikipedia, public domain): Somaliland, Ceuta, Melilla, Tristan da Cunha, the Canary Islands and Madeira. The World map's "all" map uses them too, so those six are now in its Flags mode.
- **Fixed:** Nigeria's capital is Abuja, not Lagos, on the World map too.
- **Kit updates come as pull requests.** The checks, the zips and GitHub Pages use EduGame Kit's own workflows at a kit release, and Dependabot opens a pull request for each new kit release (and for the GitHub actions). `ci.yml` and `release.yml` are replaced by `build.yml`; releases are made when a release is published (its tag must match `version` in `edugame.json`), with the package zips and the Sierra Leone data zip.
- **Played in a real Kolibri:** each package's zip (`tests/kolibri_play.py`: a Capitals round of the package's first map).
- **EduGame Kit 0.3.8.** The workflows call the kit at `@v0.3.8` (was `@v0.3.2`). 0.3.8 fixes a way a learner could get another learner's progress on a shared computer, when a learning system was slow to connect.
- **A menu on the website:** the site opens on the kit's menu of the sets of maps (`menu.html` and `menu/`, made by `edugame menu`), in English and French, with "Use it in your school". Each set has its own address (`/en/countries/`), with a "Sets of maps" link back in the header. A set gets a tick once a round in it counts: SINGE now calls `EduGame.complete()` then, which in Kolibri and other learning systems is the same "passed" as before. The menu and its files are never in the zips.
- **Releases get bundles** (`release-bundles`): one zip with every package's zip, one per language, and `singe-all-maps-<version>.zip`, the website to use offline. The Sierra Leone data zip is attached as before.
- `tests/e2e_browser.py` also plays the website: the menu, a counted round that ticks its set, and the menu in French.

### Options, languages and maps, the EduGame Kit way
- **Options** is now the kit's: a header at the top with the title and an Options button, which opens a panel over the game (Appearance and Language) instead of a separate page. The appearance shows straight away; the language changes on Apply. On the Kolibri Games games page, the header has only the button.
- **Language:** a learner who hasn't chosen one gets Kolibri's language (on the Kolibri Games games page), then the browser's. The choice is kept in the learner's save. Saves from earlier versions keep their scores but not the theme or language choice.
- **One file per language:** `languages/english.js` and `french.js` are now `lang/en.json` and `lang/fr.json`, with the kit's words, notes for translators (`_notes`) and the scoreboard and package names.
- **One folder per map:** `maps/<map>/shapes.js`, `settings.js`, its pictures, and `lang/<code>.json` for its translations (was `maps/`, `map-settings/`, `flags/` and `languages/maps/`).
- **Packages** are in `edugame.json` and built with `edugame build` (was `tools/packages.json` and `tools/build_packages.py`). Scoreboard names come in English and French.
- The World map's two variants (UN countries, everything) have their names in `maps/world/settings.js` (`variants`), so they can be translated.
- GitHub Pages is built by the kit (`edugame pages`), with each package to play.
- New `tests/e2e_browser.py` plays each package in a browser.

## 0.3.0: 2026-11-06
### Now a Scored Game (EduGame Kit)
- SINGE is built with EduGame Kit and works with the Kolibri Games plugin's scoreboards (one board per map and mode, plus an all-maps total). Existing learner saves carry over.
- **Scoring change:** a round counts, and sets SCORM "passed", when it reaches the mode's accuracy goal (0 = every round counts). Before, "passed" needed every goal in every map.
- The language and theme choices are saved through the kit.
- `lang/` is now `languages/` (the kit uses `lang/`).
### Packages
- Releases now have separate zips: **Countries** (Sierra Leone, United States), **World** (UN countries, and all countries, territories and islands) and **Continents** (not built until it has maps, starting with Africa). Build them with `tools/build_packages.py`; edit `tools/packages.json` to change what each holds.
- The World map can be listed twice with different settings (`id`, `file`, `scope` in `config.js`).
### Map display
- The zoom buttons are now at the left of the second row (swapping places with the totals box in Practice and with the skip arrows in games). The skip arrows are centred in the middle column in every mode, under the flag in Flags mode.
- Fixed the page container and attribution overlapping the map after viewing several maps and returning to the menu.

## 0.2.1: 2026-11-02
### Sierra Leone data
- **Releases now include the unofficial Sierra Leone boundary package** (`sle_admin_boundaries_unofficial_<version>.geojson.zip`) next to the game zip. (The zip was missing from the repository in 0.2.0 because of a `.gitignore` rule; fixed.)
- Chiefdom splits are now separate, uniquely named chiefdoms in the GeoJSON package:
  - Gbanti Kamarank became **Gbanti** (Karene District) and **Kamaranka** (Bombali District).
  - Nieni became **Wollay Barawa** (Falaba District), **Kalian** (Koinadugu) and **Nieni** (Yiffin and Sumbaria).
  - Mongo became **Kamadugu Yiraia**, **Morifindugu**, **Delemandugu** and **Mongo**; Neya became **Kulor Saradu**, **Nyedu** and **Neya** (all Falaba District).
  - New chiefdoms have no P-code; chiefdoms that keep their name (Mongo, Neya, Nieni, Njaluahun) keep theirs. Other chiefdom splits around the country are not covered.
- The game's district outlines are redrawn from section data (Falaba's edge differs by a fraction of a percent).
- `tools/sierra_leone.py` now holds all chiefdom splits in one table (`CHIEFDOM_SPLITS`).

## 0.2.0: 2026-11-02
### Sierra Leone
- **18 districts.** New **Bandajuma District** (Southern Province, capital Bandajuma) and **Kpanguima District** (Eastern Province, capital Panguma; also spelled Kpanguma). Both are unofficial, drawn from the best publicly available chiefdom data.
- Bandajuma is made from the Wonde, Sowa, Langrama, Niawa, Pejeh (Futa Peje), Barri, Tunkia, Makpele and Soro Gbema chiefdoms (Bo, Pujehun, Kenema).
- Kpanguima is made from the Lower Bambara, Malegohun, Yawei, Penguia, Kpeje West, Jahn and Kpeje Bongre chiefdoms (Kenema, Kailahun). Kpeje Bongre is now two chiefdoms (Kpeje and Bongre), shown as one.
- New **Jahn Chiefdom**, made from the Falley and Jonga sections of Njaluahun Chiefdom.
- Earlier changes kept: Gbanti Kamaranka (divided between Bombali and Karene), Nieni (Barawa and Wollay joined Falaba), Falaba spelling.
- Credit line and district info say the boundaries are unofficial; French text updated.

### Data
- New `data/sierra_leone/`: an unofficial, updated copy of the HDX Sierra Leone boundary package (admin 0-4, edge-matched versions, lines, points, capitals) with the changes above and a **capital for every district**. Attribution to the original HDX source and a full list of changes are in its README.
- New `tools/make_sierra_leone_package.py` rebuilds these files from the original download.

### Project
- Version numbering `major.minor.patch` (`config.js`, `index.html`, this file, release tags such as `v0.2.0`).
- README now links to the live preview (GitHub Pages).
